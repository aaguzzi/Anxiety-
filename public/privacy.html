Privacy Policy

Anxiety Companion · Last updated October 13, 2026

The short version

Everything you enter in Anxiety Companion stays on your device. We don't have servers that receive your data, we don't use analytics or advertising, and we never sell or share your information.

What the app stores, and where

  • Check-ins: your 0–10 anxiety rating, how you were feeling, suggested practice areas, and any notes you write.
  • Practice history: which practices you completed and on which days.
  • Settings: your reminder time, and the name and phone number of a trusted contact if you choose to add one.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is excluded from Android cloud backups.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Phone calls and messages

Crisis and trusted-contact buttons open your phone's dialer or messaging app. The app does not see or record your calls or messages.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete all my data in the app to permanently erase everything it has stored. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.

Children

Anxiety Companion is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical care

Anxiety Companion offers self-help practices and is not a substitute for professional care, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in crisis in Canada or the US, call or text 988. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: use the developer contact details on our Google Play listing.
